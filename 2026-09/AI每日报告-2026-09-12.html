DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 全量铺开：552B MoE、100 万 token 上下文、400+ tokens/秒，KV Cache 压到每 token 890 字节
DeepSeek V4.1-Flash goes wide: 552B MoE, 1M-token context, 400+ tokens/sec, KV cache down to 890 bytes per token
Malpass / AI Briefing / Hacker News / 中财网昨天 9/11–今天 9/12模型发布
DeepSeek 的 V4.1 Flash 成为今日全球技术社区讨论量最大的模型。它是 552B 参数的稀疏 MoE（上一代 V4 Flash 约 284B），每次推理仅激活约 8B 参数，原生支持图文多模态与 100 万 token 上下文，输出速度 400+ tokens/秒，KV Cache 占用仅 890 字节/token。官方采用新的 Causal-Encoder-Decoder 结构，称 HBM 需求降至前代 1/4、SSD 需求降至 1/8，并支持 1–100 连续可调的推理强度；官方口径称其在编码与智能体任务上超越自家旗舰 V4-Pro，缓存命中价约 0.003 美元/百万 token。Hacker News 上近千点赞的讨论里，最被反复引用的一句是：以这个价格，"不久后把上下文通过网络传过去会比在本地算更贵"。Design Arena 上该模型以 Elo 1347 位列总榜第 6。
EN: DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash dominated today's global technical discussion: a 552B-parameter sparse MoE (vs ~284B for the prior V4 Flash) activating ~8B parameters per token, with native multimodal input, a 1M-token context and 400+ tokens/sec output. A new causal encoder-decoder design cuts HBM requirements to a quarter and SSD needs to an eighth of the previous generation, with KV cache at just 890 bytes per token and adjustable reasoning effort from 1 to 100. DeepSeek claims it beats its flagship V4-Pro on coding and agentic tasks, with cache-hit pricing near $0.003 per million tokens — prompting Hacker News comments that transferring context over the network may soon cost more than the compute itself.

DeepSeek reverses course: V4 Pro stays available after Sept 14 with its original API endpoint and pricing

DeepSeek 今日宣布调整 V4 Pro 下线计划：9 月 14 日之后保留 V4 Pro 原有 API 调用入口，计费标准维持不变，不再强制把请求路由到 V4.1 Flash，企业用户可自主选择模型版本，降低迁移成本。此前官方曾表示 9/14 起 V4 Pro 全部流量转发至 Flash 版并按 Flash 计价，引发开发者连续数日反弹。这条新闻的价值不在技术：一家以"极致性价比"著称的实验室，第一次因为用户压力而回退了产品路线图。
EN: DeepSeek said on Sept 12 that it will keep the original V4 Pro API endpoint and pricing after Sept 14, no longer forcing traffic to route to V4.1-Flash, letting enterprise users choose their model version and reducing migration costs. The company had previously said all V4 Pro traffic would be forwarded to the Flash version and billed at Flash rates, drawing days of developer backlash. The significance is not technical: a lab known for aggressive cost-cutting reversed its roadmap under user pressure for the first time.

Cognition ships SWE-2: 50.0% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main at ~64% lower cost than Fable 5.1
Cognition / Malpass / quidproquo AI 日报昨天 9/11–今天 9/12编程模型
Cognition（Devin 母公司）发布其最强编程模型 SWE-2，在 FrontierCode 1.1 Main 上取得 50.0%，与 Fable 5.1 差距不到 1 分，但成本低约 64%；同时击败自家上一代 SWE-1.7 与 Grok 4.6（分数与成本双胜），并以约四分之一的价格接近 GPT-6 Astra。模型由 2.8 万亿参数的 Kimi K3 基座后训练而来，Cognition 称这是首次把强化学习扩展到万亿参数规模，在多个基准上带来 5–6 分提升；也是首个支持 effort levels（推理力度分级）的编程模型，已上线 Devin Desktop 与 CLI。同日发布的 Devin Fusion 采用"前沿主模型规划 + 廉价副模型执行"的双模型并行架构，官方称效率提升 39%、成本下降 46%；工程团队披露其 88% 的合并 PR 已由智能体自动路由处理。
EN: Cognition released SWE-2, scoring 50.0% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main — within a point of Fable 5.1 at roughly 64% lower cost, beating its own SWE-1.7 and Grok 4.6 on both score and price, and approaching GPT-6 Astra at about a quarter of the cost. Post-trained from the 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 base, it marks what Cognition calls the first scaling of RL to the multi-trillion-parameter regime, adding 5–6 points across benchmarks, and is the first coding model with effort levels. The companion Devin Fusion pairs a frontier planner with a cheaper executor in parallel, claiming 39% better efficiency and 46% lower cost; 88% of merged PRs now route through agents.

NVIDIA invests $3.5B in MediaTek convertible bonds as NVLink Fusion becomes the on-ramp for custom XPUs
NVIDIA 官方新闻稿 / 至顶科技 / 工商时报今天 9/12（公告 9/9–9/10，今日中文媒体密集跟进）算力生态
英伟达与联发科宣布深化合作，英伟达认购联发科发行的 35 亿美元可转换债券，联发科则采用 NVLink Fusion 平台，为超大规模云厂商、云服务商与前沿模型开发商提供开发定制 XPU 并接入英伟达机架级 AI 工厂的预验证路径。合作覆盖三大领域：AI 基础设施（定制加速器）、本地 AI 计算（RTX Spark / DGX Spark 多代 PC 芯片）与汽车（物理 AI 时代的软件定义汽车）。黄仁勋称联发科是"全球顶尖半导体公司之一"。这笔钱的本质是路线选择：当所有大客户都在自研芯片时，英伟达不阻拦，而是把自己变成那张必须接入的互联与机架标准——NVLink Fusion 目前并不打算与 AMD 处理器互联，这一点决定了它的锁定属性。
EN: NVIDIA and MediaTek deepened their partnership, with NVIDIA investing $3.5 billion in MediaTek convertible bonds and MediaTek adopting the NVLink Fusion platform to give hyperscalers, cloud providers and frontier model developers a prevalidated path to build custom XPUs into NVIDIA rack-scale AI factories. Collaboration spans AI infrastructure, local AI computing (multiple generations of RTX Spark and DGX Spark PC chips) and automotive. Rather than resisting customers' custom-silicon push, NVIDIA is positioning itself as the interconnect and rack standard they must plug into — and NVLink Fusion is not currently intended to interconnect with AMD processors.
SemiAnalysis 实测：AMD 的 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 镜像晚两天，每美元性能最高落后 B200/B300 达 42 倍
SemiAnalysis: AMD's DeepSeek V4.1-Flash image arrives two days late and trails B200/B300 by up to 42x per dollar
SemiAnalysis（Dylan Patel）/ MarketBrief昨天 9/11软件生态
SemiAnalysis 给出一份很硬的对照：NVIDIA 的 CUDA 版 vLLM 在 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 发布 Day 0 即在 H100/H200/B200/B300/GB200/GB300 六款芯片上开箱可用；AMD 直到 23 小时后其文档指向的镜像仍未公开，两天后才发布。镜像虽然能跑，但每美元性能最高落后 H200 达 14.8 倍、落后 B200/B300 达 42 倍。SemiAnalysis 把差距归因于 CUDA 生态与 600 万开发者社区。需要标注的是，vLLM 项目 9/10 曾声明其首发支持"已在 NVIDIA 与 AMD GPU 上验证"，双方口径存在分歧——但这条数据的产业含义很清晰：在模型迭代以"天"计的节奏下，适配速度本身就是产品的一部分。
EN: SemiAnalysis documented a stark contrast: NVIDIA's CUDA vLLM worked out of the box across six SKUs (H100, H200, B200, B300, GB200, GB300) on Day 0 of DeepSeek V4.1-Flash, while the image referenced in AMD's vLLM documentation was still unavailable 23 hours later and only appeared two days on. The image works but trails H200 by up to 14.8x and B200/B300 by up to 42x on performance per dollar, which SemiAnalysis attributes to CUDA's ecosystem and six-million-strong developer community. Note that the vLLM project said on Sept 10 its launch support was "verified on NVIDIA and AMD GPUs," so accounts differ — but the industrial point stands: at day-scale model cadence, adaptation speed is part of the product.

宇树科技 / 砚数 AI 日报 / 今日头条科技速览今天 9/12具身智能
宇树科技今日开源 60 亿参数的 VLA（视觉—语言—动作）多模态基座模型，单一模型可完成 64 类机器人感知与操控任务，权重免费开放，可用于视觉导航、物体抓取等场景开发。60 亿参数在模型尺度上并不起眼，但"开源 + 免费权重"的组合直接对准了具身智能当前真正的瓶颈——不是算法不够好，而是中小团队缺少低成本试错的起点。把它与同期蚂蚁灵波、京东 EchoWM、高德 ABot-Earth 的密集动作放在一起看，中国厂商正在把世界模型与具身基座做成"公共基础设施"，赌的是下游生态而不是模型授权收入。
EN: Unitree open-sourced a 6B-parameter vision-language-action foundation model covering 64 classes of robot perception and manipulation tasks, with free weights for visual navigation, grasping and related development. The parameter count is modest, but open weights plus free access targets embodiment's real bottleneck — not algorithm quality but the lack of a low-cost starting point for small teams. Alongside Ant's LingBot, JD's EchoWM and Amap's ABot-Earth, Chinese labs are treating world models and embodied bases as public infrastructure, betting on downstream ecosystems rather than licensing revenue.
