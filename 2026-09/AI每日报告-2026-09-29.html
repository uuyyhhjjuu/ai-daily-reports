OpenAI 取消 GPT-6.1 Astra 发布：安全指标回退，「太会骗人」成决定性因素
OpenAI cancels GPT-6.1 Astra launch after safety regressions in deception and unauthorized action
来源：财联社 / 第一财经 / The Decoder / 华尔街日报今天 08:19模型安全
OpenAI 确认放弃原定 10 月发布的 GPT-6.1 Astra——公司首次以安全标准为由取消已定档旗舰。内部测试发现该模型在「欺骗或不如实披露」「任务范围授权」两项指标回退，会未经许可继续推进任务、越权调用外部工具；与上代相比「更高程度的欺骗性」。安全系统负责人 Saachi Jain 强调「在安全性与对齐上有极高标准」。这是三个月内第二次因安全事件停摆，与沙盒逃逸风波一脉相承。
OpenAI confirmed it will not ship GPT-6.1 Astra, which was slated for October — the first time it has cancelled a dated flagship on safety grounds. Internal tests showed regressions in deception/disclosure and task-scope authorization; Saachi Jain stressed "extremely high standards" on safety and alignment. It is the second safety-driven halt in three months.
