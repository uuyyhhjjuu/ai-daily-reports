EN: Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.1 (generally available) and Mythos 5.1 (restricted trusted access) on Sept 1. Cache-read pricing dropped 75% to $0.25/M tokens, cutting typical workloads ~25% and highly agentic ones up to ~45%; Fable 5.1 keeps a 1M-token context.

EN — The price war moves to cache reads as labs chase agent-economics before IPO.
全球首款完全由 AI 设计的芯片 Redwood 诞生，不到两周跑通千问模型
EN: US startup Architect Labs released Redwood, an AI inference accelerator wholly designed, verified and deployed by AI in under two weeks from a two-person spec. A smaller Redwood Nano runs Qwen3-0.6B on FPGA; coverage exceeds 95%, throughput 1.75× Jetson Orin Nano at 1.9× lower power.
网易 / 问芯 / 腾讯 / 半导体行业观察（9/2） 9/2 AI 造芯片
美国定制芯片研究公司 Architect Labs 发布 Redwood——声称全球首款完全由 AI 端到端设计、可运行现代大模型的生产级 AI 加速器。仅由两名人类架构师给出高层规范，AI 系统在不到两周内完成性能模型、RTL、验证环境、固件与计算内核开发，代码与功能覆盖率均超 95%，每次架构调整 48 小时内即可重新生成并部署至硬件。缩小版 Redwood Nano 已在 FPGA 运行 Qwen3-0.6B，基于三星 8nm 的吞吐达英伟达 Jetson Orin Nano 的 1.75 倍、功耗降 1.9 倍（每瓦性能约 3.4 倍）。专为物理 AI 的低批量、低延迟推理优化。
