OpenAI 与 Google Cloud 宣布合作，将前沿模型与 Codex 智能体工作流引入 Google Cloud 的 Model Garden，首批包含 GPT-6 Astra、GPT-6.1 Sol、GPT-6.0 Luna 三款，面向选定企业客户私人预览，随后将进入 Gemini for business 与 Antigravity 编程环境。企业通过 Google Cloud Marketplace 采购、可用既有 GCP 承诺额度抵扣、单一账单结算，数据留在自身安全边界内。谷歌由此成为唯一同时托管 Gemini、Claude 与 GPT 三大闭源模型的超大规模云；OpenAI 强调微软仍是其首要云伙伴。含义：云的「模型排他性」彻底瓦解，企业按任务选模型成为常态。
OpenAI and Google Cloud will bring GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6.0 Luna to Model Garden (private preview) and soon Antigravity and Gemini Agent, billed via Google Cloud Marketplace and drawable from existing GCP commitments. Google becomes the only hyperscaler hosting Gemini + Claude + GPT; OpenAI says Microsoft remains its primary cloud partner. The "model exclusivity" of clouds is dead — model choice per task is now the norm.

1. 云×模型边界瓦解（OpenAI 上 Google Cloud）→ 多云/多模型编排成标配，利好中立平台与模型路由层
