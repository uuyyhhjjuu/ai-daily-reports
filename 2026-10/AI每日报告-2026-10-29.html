Anthropic S-1 逐行深读：H1 营收 163 亿亮眼，但未来支付 6372 亿、股东赤字 1276 亿才是主角
Anthropic S-1 line-by-line: $16.3B H1 revenue dazzles, but $265.6B purchase commitments + $371.6B leases and a $127.6B shareholder deficit steal the show
来源：The Plain Bagel（经 BigGo Finance 转述）/ Anthropic S-1今天 07:00IPO·深读
The Plain Bagel 逐行读完 407 页 S-1 的结论：「利润表远好于预期，资产负债表远差于预期」。2026 上半年营收 163 亿美元（上年同期 14 倍）、Q2 实现经营利润；但 2656 亿美元购买承诺（大多不可撤销）+ 3716 亿美元租赁支付，远超不足 200 亿的 TTM 营收；将可转债与可赎回优先股计为债务后，负债/资产比 1.8 倍、股东赤字 1276 亿。若按传言 1.5–2 万亿估值发行，约 100 倍 PS，且倍数建立在公司自认将放缓的增长上。治理结构亦特殊：Founder LLC 持 50.1% 投票权、长期利益信托选举多数董事。决定交易的三个变量：最终 IPO 定价、谷歌/亚马逊/微软的锁仓安排、收入能否跑赢已签约的天文数字支出账单。
The Plain Bagel's verdict after reading all 407 pages: the income statement beats expectations, the balance sheet is far worse. H1 2026 revenue $16.3B (14x YoY) with a profitable Q2 — but $265.6B of mostly non-cancellable purchase commitments plus $371.6B in lease payments dwarf TTM revenue under $20B. Counting converts and redeemable preferreds as debt: 1.8x debt-to-assets and a $127.6B shareholder deficit. At the rumored $1.5–2T valuation that's ~100x sales on growth the company itself says will slow. Governance quirk: a Founder LLC holds 50.1% of votes; a long-term benefit trust elects most of the board. The trade hinges on final pricing, GOOGL/AMZN/MSFT lockups, and revenue outrunning the signed spending bill.
