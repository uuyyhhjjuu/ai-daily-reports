对「中美 AI 差距」的冷静再评估：硬件算力（训练）美国约 16 倍优势，但评估的领先模型能力差距仅约 6.5 个月——说明算法、数据、训练方法能部分对冲硬件约束，蒸馏是关键变量。但国产替代仍受现实边界约束：Epoch AI 估计华为 2026 芯片产出算力不到英伟达全球 4%，HBM 是关键瓶颈，昇腾 960DT 提前至 2027 Q1 仍要跨过供应链与工程交付关。结论：差距是真实但非「代际不可逾越」，路径是「算法 + 国产系统 + 软硬协同」三线并进。
A sober re-assessment of the US–China AI gap: the US holds ~16x training-compute lead but only a ~6.5-month model-capability gap — algorithm, data and training method partly offset hardware constraints, with distillation key. But substitution has real bounds: Epoch AI estimates Huawei's 2026 chip-output compute at under 4% of NVIDIA's global, HBM is the bottleneck, and Ascend 960DT (pulled to 1Q2027) still must clear supply-chain and delivery. Verdict: the gap is real but not "generationally insurmountable" — the path is algorithm + domestic system + software-hardware co-design, in parallel.
