DeepSeek 开源内部推理框架 DeepInfer：兼容英伟达 + 昇腾，单集群 155 万亿 token/天
DeepSeek open-sources its internal inference framework DeepInfer, supporting NVIDIA and Huawei Ascend, serving 155 trillion tokens/day per cluster
来源：DeepSeek / 人民日报（英文客户端）/ 光明网 / Bloomberg昨天 22:00开源·国产
深度求索于 10 月 20 日宣布全面开源内部推理框架 DeepInfer 及生产级词元编解码组件 deepseek-recipe，集成高性能算子与深度优化的推理引擎，支持分布式推理、推测解码、SWA Bounded Replay 与结构化输出，同时兼容英伟达与华为昇腾。基于该框架，DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 已实现高吞吐、低延迟大规模推理：单集群日服务 155 万亿 token，整体收入约每卡每小时 38 美元。公司罕见公开了推理部署「账本」，是全球头部大模型企业中首个开放原生运行推理框架者，意味着云厂商可用接近官方的性能与成本部署 DeepSeek 模型。市场含义：从「开源模型」走向「开源生产栈」，推理成本与能耗的优化方法被商品化，直接挑战云厂商的高溢价托管定价。
On Oct 20 DeepSeek open-sourced DeepInfer — its production inference framework with high-performance kernels, distributed inference, speculative decoding and structured outputs, running on both NVIDIA and Huawei Ascend — plus deepseek-recipe, a token codec aligned with the official API. A single cluster already serves 155 trillion tokens/day at roughly $38 GPU-hour revenue. It is the first top lab to open its native running inference stack, letting cloud hosts approach official-grade performance and cost, and commoditizing inference-optimization know-how against premium managed-inference pricing.

罗姆（ROHM）协助英伟达推进 800VDC 供电架构，单机架功耗逼近 1 兆瓦
ROHM to help NVIDIA advance 800VDC power architecture as per-rack power approaches 1 megawatt
来源：21ic / 腾讯网（长江海外算力研究）昨天 14:00供电
据 21ic 报道，罗姆宣布协助英伟达推进 800VDC 供电架构，将机架直流电压从传统 48–54 伏提升至 800 伏，以应对单机架功耗攀升至约 1 兆瓦时的电流瓶颈。这是 OCP 周期内「chip-to-grid（从芯片到电网）」主线的一部分——AI 扩展已要求跨处理器、网络、供电、散热与电网的协同。对上游意味着：功率半导体、母线/连接器件与液冷供电模块的需求结构被重塑，电力而非芯片正成为数据中心部署的硬约束。
ROHM will help NVIDIA advance an 800VDC power architecture, lifting rack DC voltage from 48–54V to 800V to break the current bottleneck as per-rack power approaches 1MW. Part of OCP's "chip-to-grid" theme, it reshapes demand for power semiconductors, busbars and liquid-cooled power modules — power, not silicon, is becoming the hard constraint on data-center deployment.
