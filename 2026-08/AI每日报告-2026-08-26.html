EN: OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, its first custom inference ASIC (co-developed with Broadcom): FP8 3.4 PFLOPS, first HBM4, 4.6× H100 bandwidth at equal power — beating NVIDIA/AMD/Google parts on open-model benchmarks.
腾讯网 / IT之家 / 财联社 / OpenAI Blog（8/25） 昨天 自研芯片

EN — A自家推理 ASIC that beats GB300 per watt reshapes the inference supply chain and dilutes NVIDIA's pricing power.
