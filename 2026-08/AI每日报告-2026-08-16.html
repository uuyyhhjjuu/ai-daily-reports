💡 投资结论（中英双语） / Investment Conclusion

  • 结论一（硬件确定性最强）：大摩"芯片通胀"、野村存储重估、高盛算力缺口持续至 2027——GPU、HBM、电力设备与电网是穿越周期最确定受益环节；但需警惕"芯片通胀"本质是周期性行情，盈利上调周期见顶后或回撤。
  • 结论二（路由与治理溢价）：Stripe 70 亿收购 OpenRouter、英伟达担保砍半，显示价值链从"模型 creator"向"路由/编排层"与"风险承担结构"转移；同时 Agent 安全事件密集曝光，安全评测、可观测性、合规工具（水印/审查）成为新刚需。
  • 结论三（地缘与监管双约束）：欧盟 DMA 强令开放 Android 助手权限、白宫 Agent 预发布审查、中国苹果×阿里定制模型与面壁 A 股 IPO——监管与合规成本上升，但中国开源模型的全球规则重塑力持续增强，利好具备自主生态的国产算力与模型链。
EN — Investment Conclusion. (1) Hardware is the most defensive beneficiary: MS "chipflation", Nomura memory re-rate, and Goldman's compute shortage through 2027 all point to GPUs, HBM, power equipment and the grid — with the caveat that chipflation is cyclical. (2) Value is shifting to routing and to who bears risk: Stripe's OpenRouter buy and NVIDIA's trimmed guarantee confirm it;密集 agent-safety incidents make security/eval/compliance tooling a new must-have. (3) Geopolitical + regulatory constraints are rising (EU DMA, White House pre-release review, China's custom models), but China's open-source models keep gaining rule-shaping power — constructive for the domestic compute-and-model stack.
